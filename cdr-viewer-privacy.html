Privacy Policy

Version from the 23rd of November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the CDR Viewer application (hereinafter - the Application, we) handles your information. CDR Viewer is a viewer for CorelDRAW (CDR) files and the related CDT and CMX formats. It is designed to keep your documents and your work on your device: the files you open are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server of ours — we do not operate any. We do not require an account, we do not use analytics services, and we do not sell your data. The free version of the Application shows ads; a one-time Pro purchase removes them.

1. Data stored on your device

1.1. Everything you do in the Application — the files you open, their converted representations, measurements, annotations, and your settings — is stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded anywhere and is not accessible to us.

1.2. Exports (SVG, PNG, or PDF files) are created only when you explicitly request them, and the resulting file is handed to the destination you choose (for example, the iOS share sheet or a folder on your device). We never receive a copy of your exports or documents.

1.3. Deleting the Application from your device deletes this local data.

2. File access

2.1. The Application accesses CDR, CDT, and CMX files that you explicitly open through the system document picker or from other apps. A file is read solely to display it: the conversion from CDR to a renderable form happens entirely on your device, and the contents of your documents are never transmitted anywhere.

2.2. The Application does not request access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

3. Anonymous identifier and purchases

3.1. The Application generates a random, anonymous identifier on your device. It is not derived from your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information — we never collect those. The identifier is used solely to manage your trial and the status of your one-time Pro purchase.

3.2. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play and managed with RevenueCat, a purchase management service. We receive only anonymous purchase information (such as the product purchased and whether Pro is active) — never your payment details. RevenueCat processes this data according to its own privacy policy.

3.3. Because it is the App Store's or Google Play's responsibility to resolve refund issues, we may share anonymous information about your use of the Application with Apple Inc. or Google LLC in connection with refund requests.

4. Advertising

4.1. The free version of the Application displays ads served by Google AdMob, including banner, native, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded ads. Watching a rewarded ad can temporarily unlock paid features. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers and information about the ads you see and interact with to deliver and measure ads.

4.2. Google processes this data according to its own privacy policy.

4.3. Purchasing Pro removes ads entirely.

5. Usage data

5.1. To manage the trial period and to time upgrade offers, the Application records anonymous usage counts — such as the number of sessions, how many files you have opened, and how often you use each tool. These counts are stored on your device and attached to your anonymous identifier. They contain no document content, file names, or personal details, and are not used for advertising.

6. What we do not collect

  • No account registration, email address, or phone number;
  • No access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or location;
  • No document content on any server — your files never leave your device;
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites;
  • No sale or rental of any data to third parties.

7. Data retention and deletion

7.1. Your documents and settings live only on your device and are retained until you delete them or remove the Application.

7.2. Records associated with your anonymous identifier (Pro purchase status and anonymous usage attributes held by RevenueCat) are retained only as long as needed to provide the service. Because the identifier is anonymous, this data cannot be linked to your identity.

8. Children's privacy

8.1. The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

9.1. Your local data is protected by your device's operating system security, including device encryption when enabled. The Application makes no network connections of its own other than to the purchase and advertising services described in this Policy.

10. Changes to this Policy

10.1. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is posted on this page, and the date of the latest revision is indicated at the top. Continued use of the Application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

11. Contact

11.1. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the App Store or Google Play, depending on where you downloaded the Application: use the App Support link on the Application's App Store product page, or the contact options on its Google Play listing. Refund requests are handled directly by the store.
